Privacy

Scoperta is a simple web service for planning day trips. We keep privacy just as simple:

What we process

  • Your form input (start, destination, companions, date, pace) is sent to our server only to generate the itinerary. It isn't stored under any account — we don't have accounts.
  • The daily free limit is enforced by IP address and in your browser (localStorage). None of it is linked to your identity.
  • Shared trips get an anonymous link. Anyone with the link can view the trip.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, but only if you allow it in the cookie bar. We measure anonymously what works on the site (e.g. how many people finish planning). If you decline, no analytics run at all.

Contact

Anything privacy-related: hello@scoperta.app.

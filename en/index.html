    How it works

    1. Say where & who

      Just the town you're starting from. You can leave the destination to us — only say how far you're willing to drive.

    2. AI builds your day

      It picks the stops, plans where to eat and works out the timing so the day actually flows.

    3. Follow the map

      You get the itinerary as a timeline and a map. Share it with the others or print it out.

    Frequently asked questions

    What does Scoperta do?

    It plans a one-day trip by car: where to go, where to stop, where to have lunch and when to head back. You get a timeline of stops plus a map.

    Is it free?

    Planning a trip is free, no sign-up needed. If you want to plan more trips per day and save them, that's what the upcoming Scoperta Plus is for.

    How does “surprise me” work?

    Leave the destination empty, set a radius in kilometres, and Scoperta picks a lovely place within reach on its own.

    Does it account for kids and dogs?

    Yes. Tick that kids or a dog are coming and we favour places where they're welcome — stops get notes written just for them.

    Do I need an account?

    No. Type where you start and who's coming, and you'll have a plan in under a minute. No account, no email.
